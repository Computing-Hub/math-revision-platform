How to type your answers

Click the answer box and type. Press Enter or Check. You get 3 tries: ⭐⭐⭐ for the first, ⭐⭐ for the second, ⭐ for the third.

Fractiontype 3/4, it turns into ¾. Press → to leave the fraction.
Lettersjust type them: 7x means 7 × x
Powerx^2 makes x², then →
Square roottype sqrt
Times / divide* and /
