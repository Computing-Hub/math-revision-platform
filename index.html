Maths Arcade

Pick a worksheet, type your answers and earn stars.

How to play

  1. Pick a worksheet below.
  2. Type your answer in the box. Fractions: type 3/4. Powers: x^2.
  3. Press Check (or Enter). You get 3 tries.
  4. Earn stars: ⭐⭐⭐ first try, ⭐⭐ second, ⭐ third.

No keyboard? Use the ⌨️ Maths keyboard button. More tips in How to play.
